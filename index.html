What we're usually fixing this week

A running list of the most common calls coming out of Philadelphia homes. Click one — we'll tell you straight what it tends to cost and how long it takes.

Have one of these? Call now
Dripping faucet / valve ~$90–$180 • 1–2 hrs

Usually a worn cartridge or washer, not the whole faucet. We'll tell you if a repair makes more sense than a swap — and give you the price for both before we start.

Running toilet ~$75–$140 • under an hour

Ninety percent of the time it's a flapper or fill valve — a cheap, quick fix that stops wasting water (and money) the day we're there.

Backed-up or slow drain ~$120–$260 • 1–3 hrs

Kitchen, tub, or main line. We start with a camera-inspection option so you know exactly what's down there — no guessing, no upselling to a full repipe.

No hot water / water heater $150–$1,500 • same-day

If it's a heating element or thermostat, we usually fix it on the spot. If the tank is beyond saving, we'll quote a replacement with a firm price and do it the same or next day.

Water pressure dropped ~$160–$320 • 2–4 hrs

Could be a regulator, a partially closed valve, or scale in the pipes. We test pressure at several points to pin it down rather than throwing parts at it.

TV / shelf / curtain mounting ~$60–$150 • 1–2 hrs

Done on studs, properly leveled, with everything hidden or tidy. We bring the stud finder, anchors, and the patience to do it right the first time.

Drywall holes & water stains ~$80–$300 • varies

From doorknob dents to full panel patches. We'll match the texture, and where it's water damage, we'll flag the source first so it doesn't come back.
